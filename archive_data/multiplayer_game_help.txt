GET STARTED
Enter a name, explore with other players, and try the dungeon.

CONTROLS
- WASD: move.
- F: fire a beam of light in the direction you are facing.

IN THE DUNGEON
- Zombies move toward you.
- Face a zombie, then press F to hit it with your beam.
- A hit takes the zombie out for 3 seconds. Keep moving!

ABOUT THE GAME
This was my first "official" coding project, made with Andy.
The remaster keeps most of the original game, with a clearer name prompt, updated chat, and the zombie combat I sorta wanted to add.
