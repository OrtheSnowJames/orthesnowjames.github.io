The multiplayer game. It was my first "official" coding project. Multiplayer game was a game developed by Andy and I back in the day. When I looked back on it, I realized how much better I can code nowadays. First, I fixed up the UI. I made an actual name prompt and made the chat look better. I kept most of the old game intact for nostalgia, but I did change one thing. In the dungeon, you can actually fight zombies. I remember wanting to implement this when we were making the game, but Andy and I sort of abandoned the game and moved on. Now, I finally get the chance to "remaster" the game. The zombies go to you, and you can kill them by looking in their direction and pressing f to fire a beam of light, killing them for 3 seconds. The controls are very simple. WASD to move, and F to fire.
