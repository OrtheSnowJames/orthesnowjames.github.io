GOAL
Climb through a dark obstacle course using your grapple and flashlight.

CONTROLS
- W: jump.
- A / D: move or swing left / right.
- E: launch a grapple or pull yourself toward it.
- 1: select the grapple.
- 2: select the flashlight.
- F: turn the lights on.

CLIMB A BLOCK
- Grapple toward a block.
- Press E to pull yourself onto its side.
- Jump from the side to reach the top.

TIPS
- Hold W to keep jumping as you climb. That's my strategy!
- Keep the lights off for a harder challenge, or press F when you need a clearer view.
