Darkswing is a game where you adventure through a dark climbing course with a grapple and a flashlight. The main controls are W to jump, A and D to move/swing left and right, E to launch a grapple or pull yourself toward it, and 1 and 2 to switch between grapple and flashlight respectively. I like the dark mode. It's very challenging. However, you can always turn the lights on with F. When you grapple and press E, you can land on the side of the block which lets you jump up to the top of the block. My strategy is I hold W the whole time so it keeps jumping up.
