GOAL
Take the enemy team's flag and bring it back to your own flag.

CONTROLS
- WASD: move.
- Space: dash.

CAPTURE A FLAG
- Check your player's color to identify your team.
- Find and take the other team's flag.
- Bring it back to your team's flag at base.

FIGHTING
- Run into players on the other team to knock them out.
- Use your dash to move quickly during a chase or escape.

ABOUT THE GAME
A 2D take on Rec Room's paintball capture-the-flag mode. Best played with a crowd!
