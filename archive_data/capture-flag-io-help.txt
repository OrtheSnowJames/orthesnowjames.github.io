GOAL
Take the enemy team's flag and bring it back to your own flag.

CONTROLS
- WASD: move.
- Space: dash.

CAPTURE A FLAG
- Check your player's color to identify your team.
- Find and take the other team's flag.
- Bring it back to your team's flag at base.

FIGHTING
- Run into players on the other team to knock them out.
- Use your dash to move quickly during a chase or escape.

PROJECTILES
- Touch grenades to pick them up, and click to fire them.
- Smoke Grenades are colored grey. When fired, they explode into a grey circle of smoke.
- Grenades are colored green, and can knock players in a small radius out.

DIFFERENT COLORED THINGS
- Red rectangles are red team safe zones. Only red can go in.
- Vice versa for blue rectangles.
- Orange rectangles are "conveyor belts." These give you dash.
- Other colored rectangles are most likely walls.

ABOUT THE GAME
A 2D take on Rec Room's paintball capture-the-flag mode. Best played with a crowd!
