Man, this game. It was so much fun to code. It brings back so many memories and– Ok stop. Capture-flag-io is a game about capturing the flag and bringing it back to the base. I based it heavily on Rec Room's (rip) paintball capture the flag game mode, but in 2D. You knock people out by running into them if you're on the other team. Each person has a color indicating their team. You take the other team's flag and bring it back to your flag. The controls are rather simple. WASD to move and space to dash. It's pretty fun with a lot of people.
