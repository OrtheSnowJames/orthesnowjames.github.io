You captain a boat that starts at the shore at the top of the map and sails south, where the deeper water holds better fish. Steer with W, A and D (S reverses); on a phone you use the on-screen buttons instead. Press F while sailing to fish, but not near shops: move the mouse in circles to keep the hook on the fish (Esc cancels). Press F at a dock to step out, trade with the shopkeeper, or board your boat again. Sell your fish for coins, then buy a better rod, sword and speed, hire crew, and buy port-a-shops, one per depth level. Use the Crew button to feed your crew before their hunger runs out. Enemy ships fight you automatically if they touch you, and if you lose, your save is wiped. Beyond your furthest port-a-shop is a dangerous zone, where ships spawn constantly and sail much faster, so buy shops as you go. Days last twelve minutes, and storms come at night: a lightning strike could wreck your boat and force you to swim to land, losing most of your things. I recommend going to shore and pressing the sleep button before it happens. At the bottom of the last fishing level (around 30) is a far shore with a dock and Jonas’s house. Dock there and Jonas says he’s heading home, then you choose Complete Voyage, which counts a win and starts over, or Keep Exploring. You get three save slots, picked from the Save Menu on the title screen.
