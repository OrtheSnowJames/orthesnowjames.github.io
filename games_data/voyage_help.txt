GOAL
Sail south from the starting shore. Deeper water holds better fish and greater danger.

CONTROLS
- W, A, D: steer. S: reverse.
- Phone: use the on-screen buttons.
- F while sailing: fish, away from shops.
- Move the mouse in circles to keep the hook on the fish.
- Esc: cancel fishing.
- F at a dock: step out, trade, or board again.

BUILD YOUR VOYAGE
- Catch fish, then sell them for coins.
- Upgrade your rod, sword, and speed.
- Hire crew. Use the Crew button to feed them before their hunger runs out.
- Buy port-a-shops as you go: one per depth level. (otherwise ships will fly at you)

STAY SAFE
- Touching an enemy ship starts a fight automatically.
- Losing a fight wipes your save.
- Beyond your furthest port-a-shop, ships spawn constantly and sail much faster.
- Days last 12 minutes. Storms arrive at night.
- Lightning can wreck your boat, forcing you to swim ashore and lose most of your things.
- Tip: head to shore and press Sleep before the storm.

FINISH YOUR VOYAGE
- Reach the far shore at the bottom of the last fishing level, around level 30.
- Dock at Jonas's house. He will tell you he is heading home.
- Complete Voyage: count a win and start over.
- Keep Exploring: continue your trip.

SAVES
- Choose one of three save slots from the title screen's Save Menu.
