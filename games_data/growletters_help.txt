GOAL
Finish with the most apples. Grow Letters is also known as Grow Bananas.

GET STARTED
- Create or join a lobby.
- Set a time limit for the match.
- Click the on-screen controls to play.

THE GROWING LOOP
- Plant trees.
- Answer vocabulary questions to grow them.
- Harvest bananas.
- Trade bananas for apples.
- Spend apples on more tree seeds, then repeat.

BEAT THE RISING PRICES
- Apple prices rise exponentially every 30 seconds.
- Answer quickly and keep harvesting to stay ahead.
- Balance buying more seeds with keeping apples for your final score.
- Studying your vocabulary gives you a head start!
