Grow letters/grow bananas is one of my favorite games I made. You plant trees, answer vocab questions to grow them, harvest bananas and trade for apples. There's a problem with that last statement, however. The apple price keeps rising. Rapidly. Exponentially. Every 30 seconds. In order to keep up, you must answer questions fast, get apples, trade them for more tree seeds and keep harvesting. Only people who have studied very well can get ahead of the curve. The person with the most apples wins! You can make lobbies, join them, set time limits, and vie (vocab) for the title of the most apples. The controls are basically non-existent. Just click the things!
