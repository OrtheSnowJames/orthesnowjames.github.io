Since I made this game with advanced graphics in 3D, it's not playable on the web.
My only 3D game, the beloved Flowstate.
In this game, you fight team to team. But there's a twist! It's pool fighting!
Join a lobby through Quick Play or Join Code, then wait for the host to start the round.
There are two gamemodes: Normal and King of the Cube.
Press WASD to move around, obviously. To swim, however you have to hold Shift. Swimming builds momentum.
Momentum is used to increase the power of water moves and of jumps.
Water moves take some stamina to launch, but deal a lot of stamina damage to opponents.
There are 5 water moves available, each binded to a number key:
1: Water Power: Launches a big wave that hits harder when the opponent is far.
2: Water Attack: Launches a smaller, weaker water move that hits opponents harder when they're close.
3: Water Wall: Raises a water wall that can be lowered to deal damage.
4: Water Counter: Water Walls and Water Powers, but takes 50 stamina to launch.
5: Water Pelt: Launches a small, weak water move that can go higher up, but deals less damage.
In normal mode, you and your team have to work together to survive while fainting the enemies.
If you faint, only the lifeguard can revive you. If you are the lifeguard and faint, your team cannot be revived anymore. This is why we gave the lifeguard a paddle board and made him OP.
The game ends when an entire team has fainted. You can check the score/fainted by pressing F.
In King of the Cube mode, the team who successfully stands on the cube for 100 seconds wins.
Players respawn automatically after 5 seconds. It's recommended to use attack 5 to hit the player on the cube, as it actually deals more damage if the enemy is on the cube.
The cube team's players should try and take the hit to have the person on the cube last the longest.
