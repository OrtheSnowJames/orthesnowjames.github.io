GOAL
Team-based pool fighting in 3D. Download the game to play; there is no web version.

JOIN A MATCH
- Choose Quick Play or Join Code.
- Wait for the host to start the round.
- Choose between Normal and King of the Cube modes.

CONTROLS
- WASD: move.
- Hold Shift: swim and build momentum.
- 1-5: use a water move.
- F: check the score and who has fainted.

MOMENTUM AND STAMINA
- Swimming builds momentum, making jumps and water moves stronger.
- Water moves cost stamina and damage opponents' stamina.

WATER MOVES
- 1 / Water Power: a big wave that hits harder at long range.
- 2 / Water Attack: a smaller wave that hits harder up close.
- 3 / Water Wall: raise a wall, then lower it to deal damage.
- 4 / Water Counter: Water Walls and Water Powers; costs 50 stamina.
- 5 / Water Pelt: a small, weaker move that reaches higher targets.

NORMAL MODE
- Work together to make the entire enemy team faint.
- Only your lifeguard can revive fainted teammates.
- Protect your lifeguard! If they faint, your team loses its revives.
- The lifeguard has a paddle board and extra power.
- The round ends when an entire team has fainted.

KING OF THE CUBE
- Keep your team on the cube for 100 seconds to win.
- Players respawn automatically after 5 seconds.
- Attacking: Water Pelt (5) deals extra damage to enemies on the cube.
- Defending: take hits for your teammate on the cube so they can stay longer.
