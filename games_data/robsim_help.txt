Robsim is a game about taking money from the Blue Moon Bank. Fortunately, the bank has terrible security and an emergency exit you always take. Controlls include WASD, F and Enter. Use WASD to move, F in the bank to start the robbing sequence and Enter to interact with stuff. The bank maze is randomly generated, but the exit is always at the top center. Use the radar at the top right of your screen to see the layout of the maze and where the guards are. If a guard tags you, you get caught, your money is taken and you get sent to jail. There are stacks of gold bars in the bank you can hide behind, but they're rare. After a robbery, the people tavern react to how it went. There is also a newspaper. Legend says there's an entrance to the soup store in the bank maze. Good luck!
