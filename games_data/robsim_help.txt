The bank gets robbed once in a blue moon. Unfortunately, the whole bank is a blue moon.

GOAL
Rob the Blue Moon Bank and escape with the money.

CONTROLS
- WASD: move.
- F inside the bank: start the robbery.
- Enter: interact.

FIND YOUR WAY OUT
- Each bank maze is randomly generated.
- The emergency exit is always at the top center.
- Use the top-right radar to see the maze layout and guard positions.

AVOID THE GUARDS
- If a guard tags you, you lose your money and go to jail.
- Hide behind stacks of gold bars when you find them. They are rare!

AFTER THE ROBBERY
- Visit the tavern: people react to how your robbery went.
- Check out the newspaper.
- Rumor has it there is an entrance to the soup store inside the bank maze.
